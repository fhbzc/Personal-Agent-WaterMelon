The Play world now grows from how your days felt, shows your work as a story you can walk through, and draws every room and project by its own design; your agent learns from the times it should have spoken up, and can take the mouse when you ask.

**Play**
- **Lands from how your days felt.** Each stretch of time is read for its mood and becomes a land of its own scene - a misty lake, a snowy village, a busy market - with your agent's words to you on its title. Lands are joined by roads and vehicles designed as one network (trains, metros, ships, planes, buses and more), added to as the world grows.
- **Projects as great grounds.** A project spread over several tasks is designed as one place - open fields, a campus or one building of halls - in any outline (a circle, a star, a triangle, a heart...), its work shown as a tree, its stages and hardest steps standing out. Great pieces - a roller coaster, a tower, a lake - are drawn first, then built after their drawing, and can be ridden or climbed.
- **Every room designed, card by card.** Each card of your work is shown by a thing that is it - a clipboard for an application, a stack of pages for a paper - with progress widgets (a field that ripens, a building that rises) that change as the work goes on. Rooms choose their own walls and floors (ice, marble, logs, brick, glass and more), their own outline, and how open they are; your agent can make new things and materials and keep them for later rooms.
- **Pictures in the cards.** The map shows cartoon things; the real picture - your screenshot, or one your agent finds on the web (a paper's first page, a product's own picture) - is in the thing's card, and opens at full size.
- **Music and sound.** Each scene and place has its own music: 20 recorded tracks (CC0) and tunes your agent writes for 23 instruments, from violin and cello to koto and steel drum. Footsteps by the ground, animals and vehicles with their own sounds.
- **Your language.** Play has its own language setting; switching translates the world, and anything you look at is translated at once.
- The minimap shows the whole land; signs never overlap; the stuck button takes you to the middle of the view.

**Your agent**
- **Missed chances.** While you are away it looks back over your activity for times you needed help and it stayed silent - asking for something it could have offered, repeating the same steps, getting stuck - and learns to offer that help next time.
- **Screen control.** Say so in chat and your agent drives the mouse and keyboard for you, a red edge round the screen while it does; touch the mouse or keyboard and it stops at once.
- The side panel's report is now part of the chat; the panel can be dragged to the full width of the screen, and it stays at the right edge when you change monitors.

**Building**
- Every build carries THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt with each bundled library's license; the LGPL libraries are shipped as their own replaceable source folders.
